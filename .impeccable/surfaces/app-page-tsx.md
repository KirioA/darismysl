Scope: home landing (app/page.tsx), Persuade mode. Visitor: B2B buyer (HR, trade union, marketing, small-business owner) in Belarus. Action: send a qualified request (type, quantity, budget per set, date).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The New Year gift set as a shipment on its way: the page is a parcel and its waybill. It refuses the category default of a snowy red-gold postcard with garlands.
OWN-WORLD: The ground is kraft carton (#c58f55) and owns whole regions. Text and rules are in carbon-blue ink (#16206b). Status lives in violet-blue rubber stamps. Red ribbon tape (#d4152b) marks primary actions and crosses boxes. Waybill paper is cool white with blue ruling. Candy-wrapper prints (foil gold, pine green, ribbon red, lithographic stripes) appear only as the goods inside: the two scenario panels and the price items. Unbounded 800 for box markings, Golos Text for the form voice, tabular numerals.
STORY: This company assembles exactly my set, with my logo or for kids, and handles the documents. Filling the waybill takes a minute, then they quote.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Kraft field. Top bar: wordmark, nav, phone, red-tape CTA. Left 55%: H1 «Дарим смысл. Собираем праздник.» in Unbounded at ~6vw, ink blue; one lead line; two scenario buttons (staff & clients with logo / kids' sweet gifts). Right: a die-cut carton window with the eye-tracking mascot video, red ribbon across the box, a paper tag and the stamp «СОБЕРЁМ ПОД ВАС».
FORM: Grounded candidate 3 (waybill/warehouse paperwork), fused with candidate 1 (candy wrappers as goods) and the category canon craft bar, at the user's choice «Все». Seed key 569236e0. Raises: jackfield, where state is drawn by stamp outline (solid = done, dashed = next) and never by hue alone; neubrutalist, with a strict grid rhythm; sampler, where the order process descends top to bottom.
Signature: stamps press in (scale and rotate) as the order rows scroll into view. On submit, a «ПРИНЯТО» stamp lands on the form.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
